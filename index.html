Accounts & balances

Cash, bank accounts, UPI wallets and cards. Opening balance + movements = current balance.

Account balance rules

Transfers reduce the source account and increase the destination account. Expenses reduce the selected account. Income increases it. Credit card balances are best tracked as liability accounts with a negative opening balance; paying the bill is a transfer from your bank account to the card account.
